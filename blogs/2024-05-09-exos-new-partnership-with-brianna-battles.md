PHOENIX , May 9, 2024 – Exos , the global leader in human performance development and coaching, today announces a partnership with Brianna Battles, strength and conditioning coach and founder of Pregnancy & Postpartum Athleticism, to further advance training education and understanding for performance coaches, physical therapists and other practitioners who train clients with female physiology.
