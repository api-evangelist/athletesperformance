Santa Barbara, CA, Dec 4, 2024 – Shiftwave, the groundbreaking wellness technology company, has entered a multi-year agreement with the NFL Players Association (NFLPA), OneTeam Partners, and human performance company Exos to transform the well-being of current and former players. The partnership leverages Shiftwave’s patented nervous system regulation technology to improve athlete wellness and recovery while driving initiatives in engagement, licensing, events, marketing, and research.
