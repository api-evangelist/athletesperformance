PHOENIX, AZ - April 1, 2024 - Today, Exos , the industry-leading performance coaching company that is helping organizations reimagine the future of work, releases the results of their four-day workweek pilot. Collected in partnership with the Wharton School of Business’ Adam Grant and doctoral candidate Marissa Shandell , the data reveal that pro-recovery practices can support employee well-being and sustain workplace effectiveness.
